Accredited, and trusted by the institutions

The University of Mpumalanga and Tshwane University of Technology accredit our residences. We also house students from UJ, Eduvos, Ehlanzeni TVET College, Unigrad College, Rosebank International's Mbombela campus and private colleges around the city.

Why Ligcabho

Lifestyle and stay, under one roof

We have been housing Mbombela students since 2017. Every house is managed by our own team, not an agent: the same people sign your lease, fix your geyser and answer the phone in December.

Wi-Fi

Connectivity in the rooms and communal areas, so online classes and submissions never wait for data.

Top security

Controlled access, on-site security service and armed response at selected residences.

Work stations

A desk, a chair and a plug point of your own in every room — not a shared table in a passage.

Swimming pools

Pools inside 1 Silver Oak, 23 Dolomiet, 48 Mataffin Hill and 01 Penny Street.

Shuttle routes

Residences sit on CityBus and campus shuttle routes, or within walking distance of class.

Backup power

Backup supply at most residences, so load shedding does not end your study night.
