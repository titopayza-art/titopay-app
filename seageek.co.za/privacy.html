Privacy notice

How SeaGeek handles personal information on this website, under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Draft for legal review. This notice covers the pre-launch website only. Notices for the platform, data processing terms and the PAIA manual will be published before any product launches.

1. Who we are

SeaGeek Technologies (Pty) Ltd ("SeaGeek", "we") is a proposed South African company headquartered in Johannesburg, Gauteng. Company registration is pending. For this website, SeaGeek is the responsible party under POPIA.

2. What we collect

  • Enquiry details you choose to send, such as your name, work email, company, phone number and message.
  • Basic technical data your browser sends, such as your IP address and browser type, recorded in standard server logs for security.

This website sets no advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when it serves them.

3. Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and manage early access (with your consent, or because you asked us to).
  • To keep this website secure and working (our legitimate interest).

We won't send you marketing unless you've opted in, and every message will include a way to opt out.

4. Sharing & cross-border transfers

We share personal information only with service providers who host or run this website and our email, under contracts that require them to protect it. If a provider processes information outside South Africa, we rely on the safeguards in section 72 of POPIA.

5. Retention

We keep enquiry details only as long as we need them for the reason you gave them, normally no more than 24 months after our last contact, unless the law requires longer.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, object to its processing or withdraw your consent. Email privacy@seageek.co.za. You can also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.

7. PAIA

A manual under the Promotion of Access to Information Act will be published here after incorporation. Until then, send requests to privacy@seageek.co.za.

8. Acceptable use (summary)

When the SeaGeek platform launches, customers must use it lawfully. That means:

  • no unsolicited or non-consented marketing, and opt-outs honoured promptly;
  • no phishing, fraud, impersonation or attempts to get around verification;
  • compliance with POPIA, the Consumer Protection Act, the ECT Act and the rules of each channel, including WhatsApp's.

We may suspend accounts to stop abuse. The full acceptable use policy will be part of our customer terms.
