Something broken?

Log it and you get a ticket number the house warden works from — and the office can see how long it has been open. The maintenance book at the front desk stays the official record.

  • 12 hours: water wastage, globes, toilets, locks, unusable showers, stoves
  • 36 hours: carpets, tiles, hydroboilers, basins, taps, flush masters, shower doors
  • Emergencies, day or night: call 071 640 1574 first

Living here

The things students ask in the first week

Wi-Fi vouchers

Issued once your lease is signed, and again at the start of the second term. Ask the house warden if yours has not come through.

Room inspections

Twice a month. Complete your room occupation inspection list within 48 hours of receiving your lease so existing damage is not charged to you.

Cleaning

Cleaners come in once a day for common areas, bathrooms and kitchens — student rooms are excluded, and service is limited on weekends.

Sleepovers

R135 per person for a scheduled sleepover with proof of payment, R200 per visitor if it was not scheduled.

Still have a question?

The office is open Monday to Friday, and the assistant never closes.
