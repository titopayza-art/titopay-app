Notice to vacate

Leaving at the end of the year, or cancelling your lease early? Give notice here and the office will confirm your notice period, your inspection and your refund.

Before you give notice

Read your lease: cancelling before the end date does not end your obligation to pay, and the deposit is only refunded after the room has been inspected and your account is settled.

What happens next

  • The office confirms your notice period against your lease
  • Your room is inspected and any damages are charged to your account
  • A vacate note is issued once your account is paid in full
  • Any refund is paid after deductions, to the account you give below

Rather talk it through?

Call 013 752 4161 during office hours, or ask the assistant to have someone call you back. Giving notice is easier to get right the first time.

Your notice
Only needed if a deposit refund is due to you.

You will get a reference number and a confirmation email. Keep the reference for the front desk.

Room releases and application deadlines, before everyone else

One short email when rooms open, when applications close, and when something at the residences is worth knowing. Nothing else.
