A few things you might be wondering

Is TitoPay a bank?

TitoPay (Pty) Ltd is a South African fintech company, not a bank. Read our wallet terms and company information before you join. For questions about payments or your account, contact our team.

Is the app available?

The TitoPay app is available at app.titopay.co.za. Create your wallet today, then save it to your phone.

Are there monthly fees?

Registration, monthly wallet access and transfers between TitoPay wallets are free. Other charges may apply and will be shown before you pay.

What can I do with TitoPay?

TitoPay is a digital wallet for sending money, paying and getting paid. Check the app for the services available to your account.

How do businesses join?

Open the TitoPay app, select Business and follow the account setup steps. Contact the team if you need help.

How do I contact TitoPay?

Send us a message using the form below, or call or WhatsApp +27 72 667 1183.

Will TitoPay ask for my PIN?

No. TitoPay will never ask you to share your PIN, password, OTP, or verification code through phone calls, WhatsApp, email, or social media.
