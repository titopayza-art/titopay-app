Visiting South Africa

Using TitoPay during your visit

A trip should be about the places you visit and the people you meet. We’re designing TitoPay with tourists in mind, so everyday payments can be easier to manage while you’re here.

Carrying cash for every meal, outing and purchase can mean planning withdrawals and keeping track of change. A digital wallet offers another way to pay participating businesses, with a record of what you’ve spent.

Carry less cash

Reduce the cash you need to carry for purchases where TitoPay is accepted. Keep some backup for places that don’t accept it.

Pay from your phone

A convenient way to pay participating shops, vendors and service providers during your visit.

Keep track of your trip

See payment amounts and activity in one place, making it easier to follow your holiday spending.

Support local businesses

Use the same wallet with participating local traders and businesses as you explore.

Know the wallet fees

Signup and monthly wallet access are free, as are transfers between TitoPay wallets. Any other applicable charges are shown before you pay.
