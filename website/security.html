Security Center

Your money and information matter.

Here is how TitoPay protects your account and money, what you can do to stay safe, and how to report something that concerns you.

Our approach to security

Data Protection

We ask only for the information needed to run your wallet and meet legal checks, and explain how it is used in our Privacy Policy.

Encryption

Information moves between your device and TitoPay over encrypted HTTPS connections. Card payments are handled by Peach Payments.

Secure Authentication

Sign-in uses one-time verification codes. You can lock your profile, and we alert you when a new login is detected. Never share a code with anyone.

Fraud Monitoring

Identity verification and checks on payments and account activity help us spot unusual behaviour. Sensitive actions on our side need a second, different approver.

Privacy Commitment

You can ask what personal information we hold about you and how it is used. Contact the team through the form below or see our Privacy Policy.

Security Best Practices

Check payment details before you confirm, keep your phone and email protected, and report anything suspicious using the form below.

Best practices

How TitoPay users can stay safer.

TitoPay will never ask for your PIN, password, or verification code.

Always check payment details before confirming a transaction.

Report suspicious activity through official TitoPay channels.

Keep your device, email, and wallet access protected.

Public Security Reporting

Report suspicious activity, impersonation, or security concerns.

Members of the public can contact TitoPay about suspicious messages, fake social accounts, phishing attempts, unauthorised use of TitoPay branding, vulnerability reports, privacy concerns, or payment-related security questions.

TitoPay will never ask for your PIN, password, OTP, or verification code.

Use only official TitoPay channels listed on this website.

Submit security concerns through the form so TitoPay can issue a support ticket.

Contact Security Center

Tell our team what happened. Please don’t include passwords, PINs or verification codes.

Use this form or Ask Tito to contact the team.
