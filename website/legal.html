Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Version: Public policy Effective date: 9 June 2026 Company: TitoPay (Pty) Ltd, Reg. 2026/399418/07

This Privacy Policy explains how TitoPay collects, uses, stores, shares, protects, and manages personal information when people visit the website, join the waitlist, contact TitoPay, register business interest, or use TitoPay services.

1. Who we are

TitoPay (Pty) Ltd is a South African fintech company providing secure digital wallet and payment experiences for individuals, SMEs, informal traders, township businesses, students, freelancers, and everyday consumers.

For privacy questions, use the legal enquiry form.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to TitoPay website visitors, waitlist applicants, contact-form users, customers, potential customers, merchants, partners, suppliers, service providers, and any person whose information is processed in connection with TitoPay’s website, communication channels, onboarding, support, operations, or services.

Product-specific privacy notices or account terms may also apply to wallet, merchant, payment, partner, or platform services where those services are enabled.

3. Personal information we may collect

  • Identity and contact information, including name, email address, mobile number, business name, role, and communication preferences.
  • Waitlist and interest information, including whether you are interested in personal access, merchant access, partnerships, media communication, investor updates, or product information.
  • Business information, including trading name, business type, registration details, merchant interest, operational needs, industry category, and onboarding conversations.
  • Technical information, including IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, form interactions, referral source, cookie or analytics identifiers, and security log data.
  • Support information, including messages, enquiries, complaints, attachments you choose to provide, call or WhatsApp follow-up details, and records of our responses.
  • Account and transaction information, where TitoPay services are enabled, including account identifiers, wallet activity, payment references, merchant records, balances, limits, settlement information, dispute records, and compliance checks.

4. How we collect information

  • Directly from you when you complete forms, join the waitlist, contact support, request merchant access, subscribe to communication, or communicate with TitoPay.
  • Automatically through website technologies such as server logs, browser information, analytics tools, security monitoring, and performance tools.
  • From business partners, payment partners, verification providers, compliance providers, merchant platforms, or integration partners where permitted by law and relevant to TitoPay services.
  • From public, official, regulatory, or commercially available sources where needed for compliance, fraud prevention, merchant checks, business verification, or dispute handling.

5. Why we process personal information

  • To manage waitlist registrations, early-access communication, and product-interest records.
  • To respond to support, business, partnership, investor, media, legal, security, refund, and privacy enquiries.
  • To support product onboarding, merchant onboarding, account verification, risk checks, service communication, and customer support.
  • To operate, improve, measure, protect, and personalise the website, forms, content, accessibility, and user experience.
  • To detect, prevent, investigate, and respond to fraud, scams, misuse, unauthorised access, prohibited activity, technical issues, and security incidents.
  • To comply with legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, audit, law-enforcement, financial-crime, dispute-resolution, and recordkeeping obligations.
  • To send service notices, waitlist communication, merchant onboarding information, product education, and marketing communication where permitted.

6. Legal basis and POPIA principles

TitoPay aims to process personal information lawfully, reasonably, and transparently in line with South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Depending on the context, processing may be based on consent, contract preparation or performance, legal obligations, legitimate interests, protection of rights, public interest, or another lawful ground permitted by POPIA.

TitoPay’s privacy practices are guided by accountability, processing limitation, purpose specification, further processing limitation, information quality, openness, security safeguards, and data subject participation.

7. Sharing personal information

TitoPay does not sell personal information. We may share information where necessary with hosting providers, cloud infrastructure providers, email and communication providers, analytics providers, payment and banking partners, verification and compliance providers, fraud-prevention providers, professional advisers, auditors, insurers, regulators, law-enforcement bodies, dispute-resolution bodies, or parties involved in a corporate transaction.

Where third parties process information for TitoPay, TitoPay expects suitable confidentiality, security, purpose limitation, and processing controls.

8. International transfers

Some technology, communication, compliance, or infrastructure providers may store or process information outside South Africa. Where this happens, TitoPay will aim to use appropriate safeguards, contractual protections, reputable providers, and lawful transfer mechanisms so that personal information remains protected.

9. Retention

TitoPay keeps personal information only for as long as needed for the purpose collected, including waitlist management, support records, account records, payment records, legal obligations, audit records, dispute handling, fraud prevention, security monitoring, compliance records, and legitimate business records.

Where information is no longer needed, TitoPay will aim to delete, anonymise, de-identify, or securely archive it.

10. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion where applicable, restriction or objection to processing, withdrawal of consent, and information about how your data is used.

To exercise privacy rights, submit the legal enquiry form. TitoPay may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

11. Marketing and waitlist communication

When you join the waitlist or request product information, TitoPay may contact you about early access, product availability, merchant onboarding, service education, security updates, and related opportunities. You can opt out of non-essential marketing communication at any time.

12. Security of personal information

TitoPay uses administrative, technical, and organisational controls intended to protect personal information against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but security is treated as a core product and operational requirement.

13. Children

TitoPay services are intended for users who can lawfully use financial and payment services. Product-specific age, consent, guardian involvement, and account rules may apply depending on the service and applicable law.

14. Complaints

If you believe your privacy rights have been affected, contact TitoPay first through the legal enquiry form. You may also contact South Africa’s Information Regulator through its official channels.

15. Policy changes

TitoPay may update this Privacy Policy as products, legal requirements, security practices, and business operations develop. Updated versions will be posted on the website with a revised effective date.

POPIA Policy

POPIA Policy

Version: Public policy Effective date: 9 June 2026 Company: TitoPay (Pty) Ltd, Reg. 2026/399418/07

This POPIA Policy explains TitoPay’s approach to South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act, including how TitoPay aims to process personal information lawfully, protect data subjects, manage requests, and handle privacy governance as a fintech platform.

1. Purpose of this policy

This policy sets out TitoPay’s public position on POPIA compliance for website visitors, waitlist applicants, customers, prospective customers, merchants, job applicants, suppliers, partners, and other people whose personal information may be processed by TitoPay.

It should be read with TitoPay’s Privacy Policy, Security Policy, Terms & Conditions, product-specific notices, and any future account or merchant agreements that apply to enabled services.

2. Responsible party

TitoPay (Pty) Ltd, Reg. 2026/399418/07, is the responsible party where it determines why and how personal information is processed for TitoPay website, waitlist, support, onboarding, merchant, operational, security, recruitment, and product purposes.

For POPIA, privacy, access, correction, deletion, objection, or security-compromise questions, use the legal enquiry form.

3. Personal information covered

Personal information may include information relating to an identifiable natural person or juristic person. TitoPay may process names, contact details, mobile numbers, email addresses, identity or verification details where required, business information, application details, device and technical data, support records, waitlist preferences, transaction-related references where services are enabled, merchant onboarding records, risk and compliance records, and communication history.

4. Special personal information and children

TitoPay does not seek to collect special personal information or children’s personal information through general website forms unless a specific lawful basis, product requirement, consent, authorisation, legal obligation, or regulatory reason applies. If such information is required for a future service, TitoPay will communicate the relevant reason, safeguards, and product-specific terms.

5. Eight POPIA processing conditions

  • Accountability: TitoPay aims to maintain appropriate policies, controls, responsibilities, and review processes for lawful processing.
  • Processing limitation: TitoPay aims to process only information that is adequate, relevant, and not excessive for the purpose.
  • Purpose specification: TitoPay aims to collect information for specific, lawful, and clearly defined purposes.
  • Further processing limitation: TitoPay aims to avoid using information for incompatible purposes unless permitted by law.
  • Information quality: TitoPay aims to keep information reasonably accurate, complete, and updated where needed.
  • Openness: TitoPay aims to explain what information is collected, why it is used, and how people can contact TitoPay.
  • Security safeguards: TitoPay aims to protect information with administrative, technical, and organisational safeguards.
  • Data subject participation: TitoPay aims to support lawful access, correction, deletion, objection, and related privacy requests.

6. Lawful grounds for processing

Depending on the context, TitoPay may process personal information with consent, to take steps before entering into a contract, to perform a contract, to comply with law, to protect legitimate interests, to protect rights, to support fraud prevention, to maintain security, to respond to requests, or where another lawful ground under POPIA applies.

7. Purposes of processing

  • Managing waitlist, early-access, and product-interest records.
  • Responding to help, support, security, privacy, refund, media, recruitment, merchant, and general enquiries.
  • Preparing customer, merchant, partner, and business onboarding processes.
  • Operating, securing, testing, improving, and measuring website and product experiences.
  • Detecting and preventing fraud, scams, abuse, unauthorised access, money mule behaviour, impersonation, and prohibited activity.
  • Managing recruitment and job mail interest.
  • Complying with legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, audit, dispute, financial-crime, and recordkeeping requirements.
  • Communicating official TitoPay service, product, safety, security, and marketing information where permitted.

8. Direct marketing and communication

TitoPay may send product, waitlist, merchant, job mail, security, or company updates where permitted by law or where a person has requested such communication. TitoPay aims to respect opt-out requests and will not ask people to provide PINs, passwords, OTPs, verification codes, or sensitive secrets through marketing messages.

9. Operators and third-party processors

TitoPay may use operators and service providers for hosting, cloud infrastructure, email, forms, analytics, customer support, compliance, verification, fraud prevention, payments, communications, security monitoring, professional services, and business operations. TitoPay expects operators to process personal information only for authorised purposes and to apply suitable confidentiality and security safeguards.

10. Sharing personal information

TitoPay may share personal information with service providers, payment and banking partners, verification partners, fraud-prevention providers, regulators, law-enforcement agencies, professional advisers, auditors, insurers, dispute-resolution bodies, merchants where relevant to a transaction, and other parties where lawful, necessary, or authorised.

TitoPay does not sell personal information.

11. Cross-border transfers

Some systems, service providers, infrastructure, or support tools may process information outside South Africa. Where cross-border processing is required, TitoPay aims to use lawful transfer mechanisms, reputable providers, suitable contractual safeguards, and appropriate security controls.

12. Retention and deletion

TitoPay keeps personal information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose collected, including waitlist records, support history, recruitment records, onboarding records, transaction-related records, legal obligations, audit requirements, fraud prevention, security monitoring, dispute handling, and legitimate business purposes.

Where information is no longer required, TitoPay aims to delete, destroy, de-identify, anonymise, or securely archive it in line with operational and legal requirements.

13. Security safeguards

TitoPay aims to protect personal information against loss, damage, unauthorised destruction, unlawful access, unauthorised processing, disclosure, alteration, and misuse. Safeguards may include access controls, secure hosting, encryption where appropriate, monitoring, backups, logging, staff awareness, supplier controls, incident response, and security review.

14. Security compromise handling

If TitoPay becomes aware of a security compromise involving personal information, TitoPay will assess the incident, take steps to contain and investigate it, preserve relevant records, consider affected people and systems, and make notifications where required by POPIA or applicable law.

Security concerns should be submitted through the Security Center form, support chat, or the legal enquiry form. Do not include passwords, PINs, OTPs, or sensitive secrets in the report.

15. Data subject rights

Subject to POPIA and applicable law, a data subject may request access to personal information held by TitoPay, ask for inaccurate information to be corrected, request deletion or destruction where lawful, object to certain processing, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, and complain about alleged interference with the protection of personal information.

16. How to submit a POPIA request

Submit the legal enquiry form and choose “POPIA request”. Include your full name, contact details, the right you want to exercise, the information or record involved, and enough detail for TitoPay to verify your identity and locate the relevant record. TitoPay may ask for additional verification before acting on a request.

17. Information Officer and governance

TitoPay aims to assign responsibility for privacy governance, POPIA awareness, request handling, supplier review, security safeguards, internal controls, and compliance monitoring as the company and product operations develop. TitoPay may update this policy with formal Information Officer or Deputy Information Officer details where required and appropriate.

18. Complaints

If you believe TitoPay has processed your personal information unlawfully or has not handled a privacy request appropriately, contact TitoPay first at the legal enquiry form. You may also contact South Africa’s Information Regulator through its official channels.

19. Updates to this policy

TitoPay may update this POPIA Policy as the website, app, wallet services, merchant tools, recruitment processes, legal requirements, partner arrangements, and operational controls develop. Updated versions will be published on the website with a revised effective date.

Refund Policy

Refund Policy

Version: Public policy Effective date: 9 June 2026 Company: TitoPay (Pty) Ltd, Reg. 2026/399418/07

This Refund Policy explains TitoPay’s approach to refunds, reversals, failed transactions, duplicate payments, merchant disputes, unauthorised activity, and customer support where TitoPay payment or wallet services are available.

1. Service availability

Refund rights and procedures may depend on the type of transaction, payment method, wallet feature, merchant rules, settlement status, applicable law, product-specific terms, and the role TitoPay played in processing the payment. If no TitoPay payment, wallet, merchant, or settlement service has been used, there may be no transaction eligible for refund under this policy.

2. What this policy covers

  • Failed or incomplete TitoPay wallet transactions.
  • Duplicate payments.
  • Incorrect amounts caused by technical or processing error.
  • Merchant-related refund requests involving TitoPay payment records.
  • Unauthorised or disputed transactions reported to TitoPay.
  • Settlement or reversal requests involving business users.
  • Refund support where TitoPay is responsible under its own product terms or applicable law.

3. What this policy does not cover

  • Refunds for goods or services supplied by third-party merchants, except where TitoPay is responsible under its own product terms or applicable law.
  • Disputes caused by incorrect information entered by the user, unless recovery is possible.
  • Transactions authorised by the account holder but later regretted, unless a merchant policy, TitoPay rule, or legal rule allows refund.
  • Losses caused by users sharing passwords, PINs, OTPs, verification codes, or device access, subject to applicable consumer protection and investigation outcomes.
  • Charges, fees, or third-party costs that are non-refundable under an applicable fee schedule, merchant rule, partner rule, or law.

4. Failed transactions

If a transaction fails but funds are debited, TitoPay will investigate the transaction status. Where TitoPay confirms that funds were incorrectly debited or not delivered to the intended recipient or merchant, TitoPay will aim to reverse, refund, or credit the amount through the original payment route, wallet balance, or another appropriate method, subject to technical and partner processing timelines.

5. Duplicate payments

If a customer is charged more than once for the same transaction, TitoPay will review transaction records, merchant confirmation, settlement status, system logs, and partner records. Confirmed duplicate payments will be reversed, refunded, or credited where possible.

6. Merchant refunds

For merchant purchases, the merchant is usually responsible for deciding whether goods or services qualify for refund. TitoPay may provide payment records, transaction references, dispute routing, reversal support, and settlement assistance where the merchant approves a refund or where applicable law requires intervention.

7. Unauthorised transactions

If you suspect an unauthorised transaction, contact TitoPay immediately through the legal enquiry form. TitoPay may ask for transaction references, dates, amounts, device information, contact details, screenshots, and supporting evidence. TitoPay may temporarily restrict wallet, merchant, or account activity during investigation.

8. Refund timelines

Refund timelines depend on the payment method, partner institution, merchant process, settlement status, compliance checks, fraud review, and technical processing. TitoPay will aim to communicate expected timelines clearly. Some reversals may be immediate; others may require several business days or partner processing cycles.

9. Investigation requirements

  • Transaction reference or payment ID.
  • Date and time of transaction.
  • Amount and recipient or merchant name.
  • Description of the issue.
  • Proof of payment, screenshots, receipts, or merchant communication where available.
  • Confirmation that no PIN, password, OTP, verification code, or recovery information was shared.
  • Any relevant device, SIM, email, account, or support details requested by TitoPay for investigation.

10. Chargebacks and partner processes

Where a payment involves a bank, card scheme, payment processor, wallet partner, settlement partner, merchant platform, or other regulated participant, refunds and disputes may be subject to that partner’s rules, timelines, evidence requirements, and decision processes.

11. Fees

If a refund is processed, TitoPay will communicate whether any transaction fees are refundable. Fees may not always be refundable where a third-party cost, completed service, merchant rule, partner rule, or published fee schedule applies, subject to applicable law.

12. Abuse and fraud prevention

TitoPay may decline refund requests that appear fraudulent, abusive, repetitive, unsupported, inconsistent with transaction evidence, or connected to prohibited activity. TitoPay may suspend accounts, merchant access, payment activity, or settlement activity while investigating suspected misuse.

13. How to request a refund

Submit the legal enquiry form and choose “Refund policy question” and include the relevant transaction details. Business users may also use merchant support routes where business services are enabled.

14. Consumer rights

This policy does not limit any rights you may have under applicable South African consumer, payment, privacy, financial-services, or electronic-communications laws.

Security Policy

Security Policy

Version: Public policy Effective date: 9 June 2026 Company: TitoPay (Pty) Ltd, Reg. 2026/399418/07

This Security Policy explains TitoPay’s security principles, customer responsibilities, reporting channels, account protection approach, and safeguards for a trustworthy fintech platform.

1. Security commitment

TitoPay treats security as a core product and operating principle. The website, waitlist, support channels, wallet services, merchant tools, payment journeys, and partner interactions are intended to be protected through practical safeguards, monitoring, user education, and responsible operations.

2. Information security principles

  • Protect customer, merchant, partner, and operational information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, and loss.
  • Limit access to personal and operational information to authorised people and systems.
  • Use secure development, deployment, hosting, review, and monitoring practices appropriate to the service.
  • Monitor for suspicious activity and respond to incidents promptly.
  • Educate users and merchants about safe payment behaviour.
  • Improve controls as products, threats, legal requirements, and operational needs develop.

3. Account and authentication security

TitoPay account services may include secure sign-in, device controls, verification flows, OTP or similar confirmation mechanisms, session protections, account recovery checks, and risk-based controls. TitoPay will never ask customers to share passwords, PINs, OTPs, or verification codes by phone, email, WhatsApp, or social media.

4. Data protection safeguards

TitoPay aims to use appropriate administrative, technical, and organisational safeguards for personal information, including access controls, secure hosting, encryption where appropriate, backup controls, logging, monitoring, internal review, and operational governance.

5. Encryption

TitoPay’s security model uses encryption for data transmission where appropriate and suitable protection for sensitive data at rest, depending on the data type, system, partner requirement, and operational context.

6. Fraud monitoring

TitoPay may monitor account activity, waitlist activity, merchant onboarding, transaction patterns, device signals, login events, payment attempts, settlement activity, and support requests to detect suspicious activity, fraud, abuse, scams, money mule behaviour, account takeover attempts, and prohibited use.

7. Merchant and business security

Business users may be required to complete verification, provide accurate business information, protect dashboard access, restrict staff permissions, review settlement details, protect QR or payment channels, and report suspicious payment activity quickly.

8. Customer security responsibilities

  • Use strong passwords and protect your email and phone number.
  • Do not share PINs, passwords, OTPs, verification codes, recovery information, or device access.
  • Keep your mobile device, SIM, email account, and browser updated and secure.
  • Review payment details before confirming transactions.
  • Report suspected fraud, lost devices, unauthorised activity, suspicious messages, or impersonation immediately.
  • Use only official TitoPay contact details and channels shown on the website.

9. Security incident reporting

Report security concerns to the legal enquiry form with the subject “Security concern”. Include what happened, date and time, screenshots if safe, contact details, and any transaction references. Do not send passwords, PINs, OTPs, verification codes, or sensitive secrets.

10. Response to security incidents

TitoPay may investigate reports, restrict access, reset credentials, preserve logs, contact affected users, notify relevant partners, review merchant or wallet activity, and comply with applicable legal or regulatory reporting duties.

11. Vulnerability reporting

Security researchers should report suspected vulnerabilities responsibly to the legal enquiry form. Do not exploit vulnerabilities, access data that is not yours, disrupt services, bypass controls, or publicly disclose issues before TitoPay has had a reasonable opportunity to investigate.

12. Phishing and impersonation

TitoPay official communication will use TitoPay-controlled channels. Be cautious of messages asking for urgent payment, PINs, passwords, OTPs, wallet recovery codes, verification codes, or remote access. When unsure, contact TitoPay using the official contact details on the website.

13. Availability and resilience

TitoPay aims to build reliable systems, but no digital service can guarantee uninterrupted availability. Maintenance, partner outages, security events, technical issues, fraud reviews, compliance checks, or operational limitations may affect access.

14. Changes to this policy

TitoPay may update this Security Policy as products, infrastructure, legal requirements, operating practices, and security controls develop.
