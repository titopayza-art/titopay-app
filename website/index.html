A few useful answers

How do I start using TitoPay?

Open app.titopay.co.za, choose your account type and complete registration.

What does it cost?

Signup, monthly wallet access and transfers between TitoPay wallets are free. Topping up or withdrawing to your bank costs 3% + R2. Any other charge is shown before you confirm. See all fees.

How fast are top ups and withdrawals?

Top ups are instant. Withdrawals and payouts reach your bank in minutes, where many other providers take 48–72 hours.

Will TitoPay ask for my PIN or verification code?

No. Never share your PIN, password or verification code with anyone, including someone claiming to be from TitoPay.

How do I speak to the team?

Use Talk to Us to send an enquiry, or ask Tito to connect you with the team. You can also call or WhatsApp +27 72 667 1183.
