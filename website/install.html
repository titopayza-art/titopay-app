Save TitoPay to your phone

We chose a PWA for TitoPay because we want people to use it easily, without worrying about a big download, expensive data or a phone that’s running out of space.

You simply open TitoPay through a link on your phone. You can also add it to your home screen and open it like your other apps.

It also helps us keep costs down and make improvements faster as the business grows.

The idea is simple: make it easier for more South Africans to access TitoPay using the phones they already have.

iPhone

  1. Open app.titopay.co.za in Safari.
  2. Tap Share — the square with an upward arrow. It may be inside the More menu.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen. Turn on Open as Web App if shown, then tap Add.

Android

  1. Open app.titopay.co.za in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Tap Install or Add and follow the prompts. Open TitoPay from your home screen.

Opened this from WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook? Open the link in Safari or Chrome first. Browser wording may vary. You’ll need an internet connection to use your wallet.
