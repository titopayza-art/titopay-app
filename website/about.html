About Us

Built here. For everyday life here.

TitoPay (Pty) Ltd is a South African fintech building a digital wallet for people and businesses. We want sending, spending and getting paid to take less effort.

Mission

Make everyday payments easier.

Help South Africans send, receive, pay, shop, and manage money with tools that are secure, clear, affordable, and practical for daily life.

Vision

More people able to use digital payments.

Build useful payment tools that more South Africans can understand and use.

Core Values

What guides the product.

Trust

Be clear about fees, protect information and explain how the product works.

Access

TitoPay should work for people and businesses traditionally excluded from easy financial tools.

Inclusion

Listen to people in different communities and build around what they need.

Simplicity

Payments should be clear, fast, and practical without unnecessary complexity.

Local relevance

Start with how people and businesses in South Africa use money.

Founder Story

Why TitoPay was started.

TitoPay was founded from a real South African truth: financial access is not equal until it is safe, practical, and built for the way people actually live.

Sending money or getting paid can still mean a long trip, a queue or carrying cash. We’re building TitoPay around those everyday needs.

People should be able to support family and run a business with a clear record of their payments and less reliance on cash.

We’re working on a wallet for families, students, freelancers and small businesses. The aim is simple: make useful payment tools easier to use.

ProblemCash dependence, distance, limited access, safety concerns, and unclear payment records.
ProductA secure wallet experience for personal payments, business collections, and clearer money activity.
ImpactMore people and small businesses able to participate in digital payments with confidence.
Where Money Moves

For life beyond the city centre.

We’re building for the person running a shop, studying away from home or getting paid for a day’s work. Useful payments should reach beyond the city centre.

  • Tools for everyday payments
  • Designed for the places where cash still does the heavy lifting
  • Personal and business wallets in one app
Why It Matters

Small changes can make a difference.

TitoPay is focused on the daily realities that stop people and small businesses from using digital finance confidently.

Safety

Reducing dependence on physical cash can reduce exposure when people receive, store, carry, or collect money.

Distance

Digital payments can reduce unnecessary travel to branches, pay points, ATMs, or third-party collection points.

Business records

Small businesses need to see what has been paid and when their money is due.

Family support

Money sent between family members should be easier to track, confirm, and manage.

Youth access

Students and young earners need simple tools to learn responsible digital money behaviour early.

Economic participation

Make it easier for local businesses to take digital payments.
