About Us

Built from lived South African money realities.

TitoPay (Pty) Ltd is a South African fintech building secure digital wallet and payment solutions for people and businesses that are often asked to participate in a digital economy without being given tools designed for their reality.

Mission

Make safe digital money access feel normal.

Help South Africans send, receive, pay, shop, and manage money with tools that are secure, clear, affordable, and practical for daily life.

Vision

Financial freedom for every South African.

Build a trusted fintech platform that can support individuals, merchants, township businesses, village economies, partners, and inclusive financial services at scale.

Core Values

What guides the product.

Trust

Security, transparency, and responsible operations must be visible in every product decision.

Access

TitoPay should work for people and businesses traditionally excluded from easy financial tools.

Inclusion

The product must recognise village, township, informal, student, freelance, and SME users as first-class customers.

Simplicity

Payments should be clear, fast, and practical without unnecessary complexity.

Local relevance

The product is built around South African customer and merchant realities.

Founder Story

Why TitoPay was started.

TitoPay was founded from a real South African truth: financial access is not equal until it is safe, practical, and built for the way people actually live.

Across villages, rural communities, townships, campuses, informal markets, and small business environments, money movement can still depend on distance, cash, queues, limited digital tools, and systems that were never designed with these communities at the centre. For many people, paying, receiving, saving, collecting, or supporting family still requires unnecessary effort and unnecessary risk.

Moving money should not mean travelling far to withdraw cash, carrying business takings in public, depending on someone else to complete a payment, or operating without clear records. Those everyday barriers affect more than convenience. They affect personal safety, dignity, family support, business confidence, and participation in the modern economy.

TitoPay exists to close that gap with secure, simple, and locally relevant digital payment tools. We are building a trusted wallet and payment platform for everyday consumers, students, freelancers, informal traders, township businesses, SMEs, and communities that deserve to be treated as first-class participants in South Africa’s financial future.

ProblemCash dependence, distance, limited access, safety concerns, and unclear payment records.
ProductA secure wallet experience for personal payments, business collections, and clearer money activity.
ImpactMore people and small businesses able to participate in digital payments with confidence.
Why It Matters

The financial inclusion gap is practical, not theoretical.

TitoPay is focused on the daily realities that stop people and small businesses from using digital finance confidently.

Safety

Reducing dependence on physical cash can reduce exposure when people receive, store, carry, or collect money.

Distance

Digital payments can reduce unnecessary travel to branches, pay points, ATMs, or third-party collection points.

Business records

Informal traders and SMEs need clearer payment histories, collection visibility, and settlement confidence.

Family support

Money sent between family members should be easier to track, confirm, and manage.

Youth access

Students and young earners need simple tools to learn responsible digital money behaviour early.

Economic participation

Township, village, freelance, and informal businesses should be able to accept modern payments without losing their local identity.
