The service

What TitoPay Events is.

TitoPay Events is a cashless payment layer for live events. Patrons load value into an event wallet, spend it at any participating vendor with a QR payment, and organisers see the whole event in one reporting view. It is being built on the same TitoPay wallet, security and support foundations as the rest of the platform — not as a separate account system.

One TitoPay account

Patrons and vendors use the TitoPay account they already have. There is no second login, no separate app, and no new identity to manage.

QR-first payments

Every vendor can accept a payment with a QR code, on a phone, with no dedicated card machine required at the stall.

Live event reporting

Organisers follow sales by vendor, by stall and by hour instead of counting cash after the gates close.

Less cash on site

Less cash handling means less cash risk for vendors, staff and organisers across the run of the event.

Built for every event size

Festivals, concerts, conferences, sports events, nightlife venues, markets and private functions.

Security first

The same secure access, fraud awareness and support routes that TitoPay applies across the platform.

How it works

How cashless payments work at a TitoPay event.

Six steps, from onboarding an event to paying vendors out. Each step is labelled with its current build status.

  1. Step 1

    The organiser registers the event Coming Soon

    The organiser tells TitoPay about the event: dates, venue, expected attendance and the vendors trading on site. TitoPay sets up the event, the vendor list and the reporting view. Today this starts with the registration form on this page.

  2. Step 2

    Vendors are onboarded Coming Soon

    Each vendor is linked to the event with their own trading profile, so their sales are tracked separately and settled separately. Vendors accept payments on a phone — no extra hardware needed at the stall.

  3. Step 3

    Patrons top up an event wallet Coming Soon

    A patron loads value into their event wallet before the event or at a top-up point on site. The balance is theirs, visible in the TitoPay app, and spendable at any participating vendor.

  4. Step 4

    Patrons pay by QR Coming Soon

    At the bar, the food stall or the merchandise counter, the patron scans the vendor QR code, confirms the amount, and pays. Both sides see the confirmation immediately, so nobody waits for change.

  5. Step 5

    Organisers watch the event live Coming Soon

    Sales appear in the organiser reporting view as they happen — by vendor, by stall and by hour — instead of arriving as a cash count the next morning.

  6. Step 6

    Vendors are settled after the event Coming Soon

    After the event, each vendor's takings are reconciled against their own transaction record and paid out on an agreed settlement cycle, with a statement that matches what they sold.

Every step above describes the intended TitoPay Events service. Live event payments, event wallets and vendor settlements are not active yet, and no money moves through TitoPay Events until the service is tested and approved for production.

For patrons

How you will use TitoPay at an event.

What the experience is designed to be on the day, from the moment you arrive to the moment you go home.

Top up Coming Soon

Load value into your event wallet from your TitoPay wallet before you arrive, or at a top-up point on site. You choose the amount, and you can top up again during the event.

Pay by QR Coming Soon

Scan the vendor's QR code, check the amount on your screen, and confirm. No cash, no change, and no card details handed over at a stall.

See your balance Coming Soon

Your event wallet balance sits in the TitoPay app, so you always know what you have left before you join the next queue.

Track every transaction Coming Soon

Every top-up and every payment appears in your activity list with the vendor and the time, so the night is easy to check afterwards.

Eligible refunds Coming Soon

Where a refund is eligible under the event's terms and TitoPay's refund policy — an unspent balance after the event, or a payment that failed — it is designed to be requested from your transaction record and returned to your TitoPay wallet. Refund eligibility, windows and any deductions are set by the event and communicated before the event.

Tap with NFC or RFID Planned

Wristband and card tapping is a planned addition for events that want it. No NFC or RFID hardware integration is operational today, and none is promised for a specific event until it is built and tested.

Reporting and settlement

Where the money goes, and how you see it.

Two things decide whether a cashless event works for the people trading at it: whether the numbers are visible during the event, and whether the payout matches them afterwards.

Coming Soon

Reporting

The organiser reporting view is designed to show total sales, sales by vendor, sales by stall and sales by hour, with the transaction detail behind each number. Vendors see only their own trading.

  • Live sales totals during the event
  • Per-vendor and per-stall breakdowns
  • Hourly trading patterns across the run
  • Exportable records for post-event reconciliation
Coming Soon

Settlements

Vendor settlement is designed to reconcile each vendor's own transaction record against the payout, on a settlement cycle agreed with the organiser before the event opens.

  • Per-vendor reconciliation against their transactions
  • An agreed settlement cycle, set before the event
  • A statement a vendor can check line by line
  • Any event or platform fees disclosed up front

Fees, settlement timing and refund terms will be confirmed in writing with each organiser and vendor before an event goes live. Nothing on this page is a settlement offer or a live commercial rate.

Payment technology

QR now. NFC and RFID planned.

TitoPay Events is built QR-first because a QR payment works on the phones organisers, vendors and patrons already carry. Tap-based payments are a planned addition for events that need them.

QR payments Coming Soon The vendor displays a QR code, the patron scans it, checks the amount and confirms. Nothing to install at the stall beyond the TitoPay app.
NFC and RFID Planned Wristbands and cards that a patron taps at a stall. This is on the roadmap, not in production. TitoPay will not claim tap hardware for an event until an actual integration exists and has been tested.
Offline resilience Planned Event venues lose signal. Handling for degraded connectivity at a stall is part of the design work before any event goes live.

Can I use TitoPay Events at an event today?

No. TitoPay Events is coming soon. Live event payments, event wallets and vendor settlements are switched off, and no money moves through TitoPay Events until the service is tested and approved for production.

What does joining the waitlist actually do?

It registers your interest with the TitoPay team as a tracked request and gives you a reference number. It does not create an event, an event wallet, or any payment capability.

Do patrons need a separate account?

No. TitoPay Events is designed to run on the TitoPay account and wallet a patron already has. There is no second login and no separate app.

Will vendors need special hardware?

Not for QR payments. A vendor accepts payment on a phone. NFC and RFID tapping is planned, and any hardware requirement will be confirmed with organisers before an event, never assumed.

How will unspent balances be handled?

Where a refund is eligible under the event's terms and TitoPay's refund policy, an unspent event wallet balance is designed to be returned to the patron's TitoPay wallet. Eligibility, windows and any deductions are set per event and communicated before the event.

When will TitoPay Events launch?

TitoPay will announce launch readiness through official TitoPay channels and to everyone on the TitoPay Events waitlist. No launch date is being promised on this page.
