Help Center

Help, answers, and official TitoPay contacts.

Find clear answers about TitoPay, early access, accounts, payments, security, business services, and official ways to reach the team.

Is TitoPay a bank?

TitoPay is a fintech company building digital wallet and payment solutions. Product-specific regulatory and partner information will be communicated through official TitoPay channels.

When will the app be available?

TitoPay app availability and early-access timelines will be shared through official TitoPay channels and waitlist communication.

Are there monthly fees?

TitoPay is being designed around simple, transparent access. Any fees, limits, or product charges will be clearly communicated before paid services are used.

What can I do with TitoPay?

TitoPay is preparing personal wallet features for sending money, receiving money, paying bills, shopping, managing activity, and using future financial services as they become available.

How do businesses join?

Businesses can register interest through TitoPay’s waitlist, the business enquiry form, or the support chat. Merchant onboarding may include business verification, product readiness checks, and security review.

How do I contact TitoPay?

Use the Help Center form, Contact form, or support chat so your request is captured in the TitoPay admin portal with a ticket or chat number. You can also call or WhatsApp +27 72 667 1183.

Will TitoPay ask for my PIN?

No. TitoPay will never ask you to share your PIN, password, OTP, or verification code through phone calls, WhatsApp, email, or social media.
