Leave, notice and claims

One form for everything you need the office to action. You get a reference number by email, and the office works from the same list.

Your request
A medical certificate, a slip, a signed letter. PDF, image or Word, up to 32 MB.

Leave still needs your manager's agreement on the roster. This form is the written record of it.

Before you send

  • Sick leave of more than two days needs a medical certificate attached
  • Annual leave: give as much notice as you can, and say who is covering
  • Expense claims need the slip — no slip, no claim
  • Resignation notice runs from the date the office receives it

Something urgent?

If you cannot come in today, phone your manager first and send this afterwards so it is on record.
