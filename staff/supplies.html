Order supplies

Cleaning and consumable stock for your residence. It reaches the maintenance office as a requisition they can print and act on.

Requisition
Tick the usual items, then add quantities and anything else below.

How ordering works

  • Requisitions are collected and ordered once a week, unless you mark it urgent
  • You get a reference like SUP26ABC123 — quote it when the stock arrives
  • Count what is left before you order, so we are not storing what we do not need
  • Anything that needs a tradesman is a fault, not a supply — use Report a fault

Out of something critical?

Mark it urgent and phone the office as well. Toilet paper, cleaning supplies for a blocked drain and light bulbs in a passage are not next-week items.
