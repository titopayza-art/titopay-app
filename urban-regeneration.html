Urban regeneration

Student accommodation that brings buildings, streets and neighbourhoods back into use — and puts students at the centre of the city.

Mbombela overview

A lot of people in a small space

Mbombela Local Municipality is an administrative area in the Ehlanzeni District of Mpumalanga, South Africa. Mbombela is a siSwati name meaning “a lot of people in a small space”.

Ligcabho takes existing houses and buildings across the city and brings them back into productive use as managed, accredited student residences — keeping students close to campus, close to transport and close to the shops, instead of pushed to the edge of town.

In the neighbourhoods

Residences in Nelspruit CBD, Stonehenge, West Acres, Sonpark, Mataffin and White River — all on established transport routes.

On the transport lines

Walking distance to campus at most houses, with CityBus and campus shuttle routes for the rest.

With the community

Community churches, public parks and shopping precincts on the doorstep of our residences, not a taxi ride away.
