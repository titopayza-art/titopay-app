Student retention form

Already living with us? Tell us whether you are coming back next year. Returning students are placed before new applications, and your room is held while we check your account and funding.

Why this form matters

Rooms are allocated to returning students first. Tell us whether you are coming back, so we can plan your place.

1. Who you are
2. Where you are now
Being behind does not disqualify you — we can work it out.

5. The room you want

You will get a reference number and a confirmation email. Keep the reference for the front desk. Questions first? Call 013 752 4161, or ask the assistant at the bottom of this page.

What happens next

From this form to your keys

We check your account

The office confirms your rent account and any damages charged this year.

We confirm your funding

NSFAS, bursary or cash — we check it is in place for the year you are applying for.

We allocate your room

Returning students are placed before new applications, and room requests are matched where we can.

You sign the new lease

A fresh lease is sent to you to sign online, and your move-in date is arranged.

Room releases and application deadlines, before everyone else

One short email when rooms open, when applications close, and when something at the residences is worth knowing. Nothing else.

Accredited student accommodation across Mbombela, Stonehenge, West Acres, Sonpark, Mataffin and White River. NSFAS funded, bursary and cash paying students all welcome.
