This build — products and stock for business accounts:

  - Sales → new Stock tab: add the products you sell (name, price, category,
    optional opening stock). Giving an opening stock switches on tracking.
    Per product: Restock, Stock take (counted quantity becomes the truth,
    the variance is recorded), Price change, History (the append-only
    movement trail: opening, sales, restocks, adjustments, counts) and
    Archive (off the till, history kept). Low-stock and oversold badges.
  - Receive Money on a business account is now "Make a sale": your products
    appear as tap-to-add buttons. Tapping builds the basket, the total and
    label fill in automatically, and generating the payment QR records the
    sale — tracked stock counts down on the server, priced server-side.
    Overselling is refused unless the till explicitly confirms (then the
    count goes negative until the next stock take corrects it).
  - New API endpoints under /v1/business/products/* (business accounts
    only; ownership enforced on every movement).
