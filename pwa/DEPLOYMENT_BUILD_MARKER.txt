Also in this build — the jumping is fixed at the source, and the footer is flat:

  THE TWO REASONS THE APP FELT JUMPY, both now fixed for every screen at
  once rather than one sheet at a time:

  1. Dismissing the keyboard threw you to the top of the sheet. The code
     that returns a sheet to the top when a field loses focus was written
     for the one-screen sign-in panel — but it was attached to EVERY
     sheet containing an input. On a long form (limits, goals, a stock
     take, paying for a need) closing the keyboard sent you back to the
     start. It now applies to sign-in only.

  2. Every save, toggle or delete threw you to the top. Almost all sheets
     refresh by calling openModal again with new HTML, which tears out
     the old card and builds a new one — losing the scroll position every
     time. openModal now carries your position across when it is the SAME
     sheet being re-rendered, and still starts at the top when it is a
     different one. Ticketing had a hand-written patch for this; it is
     now a single rule that covers all 150 sheets.

  Both are held by verification/no-jump.js, which drives a real browser:
  same sheet re-rendered keeps the position, a different sheet starts at
  the top, and dismissing the keyboard leaves a long form where it was.
  Each was verified by putting the old behaviour back and watching the
  test fail.

  THE FOOTER is now flat: a solid white bar across the whole bottom,
  square edges, no translucency or blur, with the middle action seated in
  a rounded notch cut into the bar rather than floating over it.

Also in this build — a wristband button that could vanish without saying why:

  "Link Event Tag" was never removed. It appears when the server lists a
  ticket as linkable: the ticket is valid, its event is approved AND
  cashless, and no tag is on that ticket yet. When one of those is not
  true the button is correctly absent.

  What was wrong is what happened when the LOOKUP FAILED. Both tag calls
  were wrapped in a catch that returned an empty list, so a 500, an
  expired session or a weak signal produced exactly the same screen as
  "this event is not cashless" — no button, no message, no way to tell
  the two apart. Someone who knows the feature exists is left thinking it
  was taken away.

  My Tickets now says so: "Wristbands could not be checked — your tickets
  are fine and unchanged", with Try again. The tickets themselves are
  never held back by a tag failure.

  IF THE BUTTON IS STILL MISSING with no such notice, the event is not
  cashless for that ticket. On the business side: Ticketing → Event Tags →
  Cashless → pick the event → On → Save cashless setting. Then reopen My
  Tickets.

Previous build — organisers can export the tags they just issued:

  EVENT TAGS: the panel that lists freshly issued blank tags now offers

TitoKids co-parents:

  Raising a child is rarely a solo job, and neither is paying for one. A
  parent can now invite another adult — the other parent, a guardian, a
  grandparent — to help manage ONE child's wallet. It is per child, never
  per family: being trusted with one child's money says nothing about
  another's.

  THE MONEY ANSWER, said plainly on the screen: a co-parent adds money
  from THEIR OWN wallet. They can never spend the parent's. This is not a
  policy in code comments — funding debits whoever performs it, and the
  live harness proves the owner's balance does not move when a co-parent
  funds.

  What a co-parent can do: add money from their own wallet, pay for a
  need, set limits and category switches, and answer the child's requests
  (approving funds it from their own wallet). Both approvers are alerted
  when a child asks; whoever answers first settles it.

  What stays with the parent who set the wallet up: inviting and removing
  co-parents, and removing the child.

  An invitation must be ACCEPTED. Until then it grants nothing — the
  child stays invisible to the invitee. Invitations appear at the top of
  TitoKids with Accept and Decline, and arrive by in-app alert and email.
  Either side can end the arrangement, and access stops immediately.
  The child can never be made a manager of their own limits.

Proven: verification/titokids-live.js 15/15, six of them new — strangers
