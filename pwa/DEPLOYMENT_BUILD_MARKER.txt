This build — "I added them as a manager and nothing appears":

  THE CAUSE. A staff member only reaches a person when the contact typed
  matches an ACTIVE TitoPay account. When it does not, the entry is still
  saved — deliberately, a business may list someone before they join —
  but nothing is sent and My Workplaces stays empty on their phone. The
  only sign of it was one toast at the moment of adding, and the register
  afterwards looked identical either way. The owner believed the person
  was added; the person saw an empty screen; nobody could see why.

  THE FIX.
  - The Staff register now SHOWS the state on every row: "Linked to
    @username — can sell for you", or an amber row reading "Not linked —
    no TitoPay account matches this contact, so they were not notified
    and cannot sell yet".
  - Unlinked rows carry a Link button. It retries on the stored contact
    or a corrected one, and is also the flow for "they have joined
    TitoPay since I added them". A wrong contact is refused with the
    reason and the attempt is remembered, so nobody retypes it.
    Linking notifies the person immediately and they can sell at once.
  - A contact that is already on the register under another name is
    refused with that name, instead of failing on a database constraint.
  - My Workplaces, when empty, now tells the staff member exactly what to
    do: send the business your @username (shown, so it can be copied) and
    ask them to tap Link next to your name.

  SECURITY (api.zip): node-forge 1.3.1 -> 1.4.0, clearing the one
  high-severity advisory npm audit reported. It signs Apple Wallet
  passes, so it was verified rather than assumed — the pass tests build a
  real certificate, run the real signing pipeline and parse the PKCS#7
  back. npm audit now reports 0 vulnerabilities.
