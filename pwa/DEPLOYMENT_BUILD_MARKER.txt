This build — the Services wall becomes a page, and the app stops
borrowing the browser's voice:

  SERVICES ARE GROUPED. Nineteen tiles in one flat grid was a list you
  had to read. The same tiles now sit under headings — Send & pay, Money
  in & out, Buy, Plan & save, Run your business, More — so the screen can
  be scanned. Nothing moved further away: every service is still exactly
  one tap from that screen, opening the same way it always did.
  "More" is a catch-all that owns nothing. A service added to the
  catalogue that nobody grouped lands there rather than disappearing, so
  grouping can never lose a feature.

  THE APP ASKS IN ITS OWN VOICE. Every window.prompt and window.confirm
  is gone — 16 of them. Stock counts, restocks, price changes, a meeting
  title and closing feedback were collected through browser prompts, and
  archive/revoke/remove confirmed through browser confirms. On a phone
  those arrive as a grey box carrying the site's address, which reads as
  "the app broke" rather than "the app asked". They are now TitoPay's own
  dialog, laid OVER the sheet you are working in and leaving it exactly
  as it was. Escape and tapping outside cancel; Enter accepts a typed
  value; destructive steps get a red button.

  A destructive button now looks destructive: the --danger token existed,
  the .btn.danger variant did not.

  Event Scanners: the rename now also reaches the offline fallback
  catalogue the app uses when it cannot read the server.

  SIMPLICITY.md gains the two new rules, and
  api/test/app-navigation-simplicity.test.js enforces them — it fails if
  a browser prompt returns, if the dialog is ever built on openModal (it
  would close the sheet underneath), if Escape stops being captured, if a
  service is claimed by two groups, or if "More" stops being the
  catch-all.
