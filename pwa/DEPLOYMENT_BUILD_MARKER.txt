This build — TitoKids goes live, and the app gets simpler:

  TITOKIDS (new, under Personal):
  - A child gets a real ring-fenced wallet under the parent's account.
    Every movement is ordinary double-entry ledger, so balances can never
    drift from the money that actually moved.
  - Add Money moves funds from the parent's wallet to the child's.
    Pay for a Need pays a school, shop or person straight from the
    child's wallet — categorised, no loose cash.
  - Limits & Controls: category switches plus daily, weekly and monthly
    caps. A switched-off category is refused outright. A cap asks the
    parent to confirm before paying anyway, and the override is audited.
  - Savings goals, with progress measured from the ledger.
  - A child with their own TitoPay can be linked: they see their balance
    and activity, and can ask the parent for money. The parent approves
    or declines in the same sheet, and approving performs the transfer.
  - A child holding money cannot be removed — the balance must be moved
    or spent first.
  - Communication is by in-app alert and email only, never SMS.

  SIMPLER NAVIGATION (nothing removed, duplicate doors closed):
  - One door per feature. TitoKids, Sales and My Family no longer appear
    twice; each now has exactly one home. My Family is not a separate
    screen at all — a linked child's wallet appears inside the TitoKids
    sheet, above the children they manage.
  - Bulk Distribution shows in Profile only while its tile is hidden, so
    an approved business sees it once, not twice.
  - App search now covers every feature that lives behind a single door
    — TitoKids, My Workplaces, TitoPay Chat, Sales, Bulk Distribution —
    so closing a duplicate door never made anything harder to find.
  - "Ticketing Staff" is renamed "Event Scanners". It kept colliding with
    the business Staff register, which is a different thing entirely.
    Existing installations pick up the new name automatically.

  FIXED: dates that carry a time — TitoKids activity, stock movements,
  workplace start dates, a staff member's last scan — displayed as
  "Not set". They now show the real date.

Proven: verification/titokids-live.js 9/9 — a linked child added and
alerted, funding conserved to the cent, a switched-off category refused,
a daily cap refused then paid on confirmation with the override audited,
a goal measured from the ledger, a request approved moving real money, a
declined request moving none, no double decisions, strangers blind to
the child, and a funded child protected from removal. Full API suite
459/459. Bundle boots clean in Chromium; the merged TitoKids sheet was
rendered and checked.

Migrations to run: none — tables create themselves on first use (also in
