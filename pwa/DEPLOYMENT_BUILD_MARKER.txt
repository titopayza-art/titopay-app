WHAT CHANGED IN THE APP

1. BUSINESS VERIFICATION IS ITS OWN SCREEN.
   Profile > Grow your business > Business Verification, on business
   accounts. It has two clearly separate halves:

     AUTHORISED PERSON   read, never re-collected. Your identity was
                         verified once, on your own record, and the screen
                         says so.
     BUSINESS ENTITY     the business's own name, type and registration
                         number, and your ROLE at it (owner, director,
                         beneficial owner, authorised representative,
                         partner, trustee, member).

   A sole proprietor or informal trader is never shown a registration
   number field. A registered company must supply one, and a 13 digit
   personal identity number is refused as a business identifier.

   Add as many businesses as you are genuinely authorised on. Each carries
   its own name, registration number, verification status and audit trail.

2. VERIFY YOUR IDENTITY IS UNCHANGED.
   Same layout, same typography, same colours, same document selector, same
   SA ID field, same passport and other approved document route, same
   behaviour. No company registration number was added to it, and none ever
   will be: it verifies a natural person.

3. THE VERIFICATION LEVELS SCREEN SHOWS THREE LEVELS AND THE NEW LADDER.

     Unverified       R25 000 a month, R2 500 per payment
     Basic verified   R200 000 a month, R10 000 per payment
     Fully verified   "No fixed monthly transaction limit. Risk assessment,
                      transaction monitoring and applicable TitoPay
                      compliance requirements still apply."

   The monthly figure is a transaction VOLUME limit. The per-payment, per-day,
   withdrawal and wallet-balance numbers on the Limits screen are separate
   concepts with their own values, and they did not move with it.

   Every number is read from the API, so nothing is hard-coded in the app
   and changing a limit in the console changes the screen with no upload.

   The word "unlimited" is deliberately not used for the top level, and a
   test fails the build if the limit copy ever uses it. The engine applies
   risk LAST, so an account whose risk banding has been raised still gains
   real ceilings at that level. "No fixed monthly transaction limit" is what
   actually happens; "unlimited" would be a promise the platform is built not
   to keep.

   Enhanced due diligence still appears below the three levels, still
   described as a review rather than a level anyone climbs to.

NOTHING ELSE MOVED

   No other screen, colour, spacing, icon, button, animation or navigation
   path changed. The wallet, transfers, QR payments, tickets, stokvels,
   TitoKids, merchant, staff and rewards flows are untouched.

UPLOAD NOTES

   Replace the whole app directory as usual. The asset stamp moves to
   ?v=408 in index.html and service-worker.js and the cache name becomes
   titopay-pwa-v408-business-verification, so no customer can be served a
   cached app.min.js.

   REQUIRES api.zip build 30. On an older API the Business Verification
   tile opens and reports that it could not load, because
   /v1/business/verification does not exist there. Nothing else is
   affected.
