## Full description (max 4000 characters)

TitoPay is South Africa's smart wallet for people and businesses. Send money
in seconds, pay by QR at the till, split bills with friends, save with your
stokvel, and buy tickets to events near you — all from one app.

**FOR YOU**
- Send and receive money instantly with a username, cellphone number or QR code
- Pay any TitoPay business by scanning their QR — see the verified name and the
  exact fee before you confirm
- Request money and split bills, with everyone's share worked out to the cent
- Save together: create or join a stokvel with contributions, group chat and a
  transparent record every member can see
- TitoKids: give your children their own supervised wallet with limits you set
- Buy event tickets with QR entry, and email or gift a ticket to a friend
- Download statements and proof of account whenever you need them

**FOR YOUR BUSINESS**
- Accept payments with your own QR code — on a poster, at the till, anywhere
- Make a Sale: ring up products, take payment and track stock from your phone
- Sell event tickets with entry scanning, staff selling and sales reports
- Payouts to your bank account, sales summaries and business statements

**BUILT SAFE**
- FICA-compliant identity verification with clear account tiers
- Encrypted connections everywhere (HTTPS/TLS), for you and for the systems we
  work with
- Optional 2-step sign-in: add an emailed one-time code to every login
- Wallet lock the moment something feels wrong — money in still arrives, money
  out stops
- Verified recipients and exact fees shown before every payment; protection
  against duplicate taps
- We never ask for your PIN, password or OTP by phone, email or WhatsApp

**HONEST WITH YOUR MONEY**
- See the fee before you pay, every time
- Your transaction history, statements and limits are always visible in the app
- Close your account whenever you choose, from Security Centre — reviewed by
  our team, with your money returned to you first

TitoPay is built in South Africa, for South Africa. Questions? Reach us in the
app under Support, or visit titopay.co.za.

## Data safety form (Google Play) — truthful answers
- Collects: name, email address, phone number, financial info (transactions),
  user IDs. Photos only if the customer adds a profile photo.
- Data is encrypted in transit: YES (TLS everywhere).
- Users can request deletion: YES (in-app closure request + web page).
- Data shared with third parties: payment processing partners for the purpose
  of processing payments (list your live providers when filling the form).
- No advertising SDKs, no location collection, no contacts upload.
