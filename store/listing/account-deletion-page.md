## Closing your TitoPay account

You can ask us to close your TitoPay account at any time, directly from the
app:

1. Sign in to TitoPay.
2. Open **Security Centre** from your profile.
3. Tap **Close TitoPay Account** and confirm your request.

Before your account closes:

- **Withdraw or transfer any money in your wallet first.** We will not close
  an account that still holds funds — your money always comes back to you.
- Our support team reviews every closure request. We may contact you to
  confirm it was really you, and you can cancel the request in the app at any
  time before it is approved.

When your closure is approved, sign-in is disabled on every device
immediately.

**What we keep, and why.** South African law (including the Financial
Intelligence Centre Act) requires us to retain certain identity and
transaction records for a legally mandated period after an account closes. We
keep only what the law requires, protect it the same way we protect active
accounts, and delete it when the retention period ends.

Can't access the app? Email us from your registered address at
[support email] with the subject "Account closure request" and we will start
the process for you after verifying your identity.
