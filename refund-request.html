Ask for a refund

Paid twice, paid too much, or owed your deposit back? Send the claim here with your proof of payment and your banking details, and the office will work through it.

Your claim
Banks reject payments where the name does not match the account.
In rands, figures only.
On the email we sent when you applied.
Where must the money go?
PDF or a photo of the bank confirmation. It is the fastest way to get the claim approved.

You will get a reference like REF26ABC123, and an email at every step.

What happens next

  • Finance matches your claim to the payment in our records
  • Deposit refunds are only paid after the room has been inspected and the account settled
  • Payment goes into the account you give here, in the account holder's name
  • We email you when the claim is approved and again when it is paid

Please note

The administration fee paid to reserve a room is not refundable, whether or not the application is successful. Everything else that was over-paid or paid twice is.

Chasing a claim?

Quote your REF number. Call 013 752 4161 during office hours, or ask the assistant to have someone call you back.

Room releases and application deadlines, before everyone else

One short email when rooms open, when applications close, and when something at the residences is worth knowing. Nothing else.

Accredited student accommodation across Mbombela, Stonehenge, West Acres, Sonpark, Mataffin and White River. NSFAS funded, bursary and cash paying students all welcome.
