About us

Ligcabho Le'Africa Residences was established in 2017 to meet the growing need for student accommodation in the city of Mbombela.

Who we are

Both lifestyle and stay

Ligcabho Student Accommodation prides itself on offering both lifestyle and stay, providing accommodation for male and female students studying around Mbombela city. We accept NSFAS funded students, bursary holders and cash paying students.

Ligcabho was established in 2017 to meet the growing need for student accommodation in the city of Mbombela. We aim to offer students a safe, convenient and affordable place to live that meets all of a student's needs while developing students and shaping their greater minds. We are not just for stay — we have more ways to keep students entertained, with plenty of activities to help students relax and live better.

Our vision

To become Mbombela's largest and leading independent provider of student accommodation in town.

How we run

A team behind every house

Manager

Responsible for the full operation of the building, and reports to the board.

Admin and house warden

Keep the building maintained, clean, safe and secure, and are the first point of contact for students.

Maintenance

Service providers on call for all maintenance issues, with published turnaround times.

Cleaners

In once a day for common areas, bathrooms and kitchens.

Security

A security service provider with personnel in place to attend to all security matters.

Student house committee

Two elected student representatives per residence, reporting to the house warden.
