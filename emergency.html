Who to call

Our 24 hour line. Call it — and if you cannot speak, WhatsApp the same number and say where you are. This is the only thing we use WhatsApp for; everything else goes through the assistant on the site, the forms, or the office.

After hours

Admin and the house warden are not available after 20h00 unless the building or someone's life is in danger — in that case, call immediately.
