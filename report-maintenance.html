Report a fault

Something broken in your room or in the building? Log it here and you get a ticket number the house warden works from.

If it is an emergency, phone first

Fire, flooding, gas leak, exposed wiring or danger: call 071 640 1574. Log it here afterwards.

How quickly things get fixed

  • 12 hours: water wastage, globes, toilets, locks, unusable showers, stoves
  • 36 hours: carpets, tiles, hydroboilers, basins, taps, flush masters, shower doors
  • Logged after 15h00 on a Friday, it may only be attended to on Monday unless it is an emergency
  • Damage caused by students is charged to the student's account
